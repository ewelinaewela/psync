# Code of Conduct

Psync is committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

- communicate with empathy and patience;
- give specific, useful feedback;
- respect different backgrounds, abilities and experiences;
- protect personal and health-related information;
- accept maintainer decisions made to preserve safety and project scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information and repeated disruptive conduct are not accepted in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behaviour conflicts with these expectations.
